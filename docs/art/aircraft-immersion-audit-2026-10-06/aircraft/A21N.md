## Most consequential mismatches

The A321neo should gain length rather than lose cabin width. Airbus gives A321neo cabin length 34.44 m versus A320ceo 27.51 m, with both maximum cabin widths 3.70 m ([A321neo](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/en/aircraft/a320-family/a321neo), [A320ceo](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/en/aircraft/a320-family/a320ceo)). Yet profile half-widths are 1.789 versus 1.892. The shared seat-width formula consequently gives approximately 0.490 versus 0.524 m before cushion clearance (`game/Airside/Assets/Airside/Presentation/PassengerCabinInterior.cs:49–64`). This is a calibration warning, not proof that either number represents the real cabin section: maximum width differs from width at window height, and lining thickness matters.

Both cabins are the same five-row, 3.95 m-long box, with bulkheads just 1.975 m fore/aft of the eye (`PassengerCabinInterior.cs:38–47`). Moving the window station alone cannot convey the A321's longer aisle. Windows share a 0.32 × 0.44 m elliptical opening and 45 mm reveal (`:92–114`), without type-specific bezel or shade geometry. The historical dark corners warrant a future aperture/normal/material review; the fixture alone cannot identify their present cause.

Cockpit sharing is appropriate: `JetCockpitProfile.cs:24–25` uses `AirbusClassic`, equal 1.24 half-width, but separate eye fits. Airbus confirms A320-family common cockpit design ([Cockpits](https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/commercial-aircraft/cockpits)). Detail deficiencies belong mainly to that shared family. `JetCockpitInterior.cs:64–69,120–131,152–158` constructs straight grips, rails, panel boxes and generic guidance controls. Do not invent a wholly different neo cockpit.

Exterior provenance also deserves care: `scripts/generate-air-008-a321neo.py:23–50` scales inherited 737-8 topology to the A321neo envelope and removes lower tips. Current `game/Airside/Assets/Airside/Art/Models/Aircraft/mdl_a321neo_v01.gltf` includes subsequent glazing/mask/pilot additions, so that generator alone is not a complete account of today's asset. Matching bounds does not establish Airbus nose, door, nacelle or wing station fidelity.

## Prioritised proposals

1. **P1 — Calibrate the A21N cross-section and stations.** Compare current hull section, lining clearance, window centres and seated eye with manufacturer drawings. Preserve 3+3 and select representative seat dimensions before adjusting either profile width. Player sees credible shoulder space and wing position. Shared profile contract; A21N-specific fit. Impact high / effort M.
2. **P1 — Replace the conspicuous passenger rim.** Build a layered, softly squared oval bezel, curved lining return and stowed shade; resolve exposed corner voids on both sides. Keep the wing opening clear. Shared Airbus-family component with A21N station parameters. Impact high / M.
3. **P1 — Add bounded longitudinal depth.** Continue aisle/bin modules beyond the local five-row section and place distant partitions according to an explicitly chosen fictional layout. Show A321 length during inward glances without stretching seats, bins or cockpit. Shared modular builder; A21N depth/station data. Impact high / M.
4. **P2 — Refine the shared Airbus flight deck.** Shape sidestick grips, sculpt consoles/tray housings, give FCU knobs and overhead clusters differentiated silhouettes, soften windscreen transitions. Preserve existing display roles and spectator readouts. A320-family ownership; A21N eye calibration retained. Impact high / M.
5. **P2 — Choose and document cabin generation.** Airspace may supply integrated shades, slimmer sidewalls, larger bins and LED lighting; Airbus documents an A321neo operator choosing it, not every neo having identical furnishings ([Airspace service entry](https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2021-09-airbus-new-single-aisle-airspace-cabin-enters-into-service-with)). Make this a fictional operator design choice. Shared family option; A21N configuration. Impact medium / M.
6. **P2 — Refine exterior identity geometry locally.** Check Airbus nose/glazing outline, chosen CFM/PW nacelle variant, sharklets and longitudinal door/window arrangement against official references; retain current paint. Do not rescale the whole inherited mesh again. A21N model ownership. Impact medium / L.
7. **P3 — Tune framing and lighting after geometry.** Current passenger/cockpit entry shares 65° FOV (`AirsideCameraController.Cockpit.cs:45–83`); offer a seated window composition with adjacent seat/bezel context. Use restrained lit plastic/fabric differences and gentle ceiling illumination. Shared camera/materials, A21N pose. Impact medium / S–M.

## First pass and acceptance

Start with cross-section/station calibration, the window assembly, then longitudinal depth. Together these correct the strongest passenger-view weaknesses while retaining Airbus family consistency.

Implementation should record measured mesh sections and eye/window clearances; verify six-seat grouping, A21N-specific stations, independent seat/window spacing, plausible distant partitions, and unchanged exterior bounds. Preserve wing/engine visibility (`CockpitExteriorVisibility.cs:12–24`) and interior activation lifetime. These are proposed checks, not checks run here.

Future visual acceptance requires current player captures of cockpit forward/side/down, both passenger windows and inward aisle glances, and exterior orbit; day/dusk/night, banking and takeoff/climb. Review for corners, clipping, excessive glare, scenery visibility and frame cost. HUD/readout overlap in the latest flight-information changes remains unknown. No such runtime review was performed.
