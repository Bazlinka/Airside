## Largest mismatches

**A320 recognition stops at the controls.** `JetCockpitProfile.cs:14–25` correctly selects AirbusClassic, sidesticks and six displays. `JetCockpitInterior.cs:62–69,162–171` supplies two side grips, trays, paired PFD/ND and stacked centre displays. The old fixture shows these, but the beam grip, slab console and mostly flat panel remain crude. The guidance strip uses eight repeated knobs (`:154–159`); overhead sections repeat a generic toggle grid (`:276–287`). Airbus confirms the side-stick and common A318/A319/A320/A321 cockpit; this supports shared family geometry, not applying every modern feature to a ceo.

**Windscreen silhouette is generic.** `JetCockpitInterior.cs:93–94,120–131` calls a width-only shared shell and gives nearly all jets the same rails and quarterlight. `JetCockpitShellGeometry.cs:24–35` shares sill/brow/eye-relative datums. The old A320 fixture consequently reads as a broad rectangular window belt. Exact A320 pane contours and captain-eye visibility should drive a family shell, while retaining the existing fitted A320 eye datum. Airbus’s official cockpit-photo link failed to fetch; this audit does not claim a measured photo match.

**Passenger apertures and seats have independent rhythms.** `PassengerCabinProfile.cs:22` sets half-width 1.892, window station −13.960, pitch 0.79 and 3+3 seats. `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:47,94–114` repeats windows at seat pitch and uses the same 0.32 × 0.44 m ellipse for every aircraft. The exterior generator instead repeats A320 windows at 0.67 m (`scripts/generate-air-adelaide-fleet.py:156–163`). Aligning one selected window does not prove adjoining openings agree. These are model parameters, not certified window dimensions.

**The cabin section is a box.** `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:38–64` uses a flat ceiling, end slabs, box bins and seats. Airbus’s A320-200-applicable manual illustrates six-abreast economy configurations and a curved cross-section; it does not establish one mandatory operator seat pitch. The A21N profile is 1.789 m half-width (`PassengerCabinProfile.cs:23`), 0.103 m smaller despite family commonality. Investigate local mesh stations and lining clearance before changing either: these values fit authored exterior kits and are not evidence of a real family cabin-width difference.

## First pass and acceptance

Start with priorities 1–3. They address aircraft identity and the frame occupying most of the player’s screen without requiring functional avionics or a whole-aircraft remodel.

Implementation should document transformed A320 window bounds, seat/window spacing, lining clearance and eye datum; compare A21N before copying width. Retain 3+3 identity, ceo engine scope and mesh/material batching. `CockpitInterior.cs:25–56` hides/restores exterior renderers; `CockpitExteriorVisibility.cs:12–23` retains wings, controls and engines. Preserve those lifetimes and the shared flight HUD.

Future visual acceptance, **not performed here**: packaged forward/side/down/overhead cockpit review; left/right outward and aisle glances at recentered and extreme permitted gaze; wing/engine continuity; complete exterior restoration after repeated view switches; daylight/dusk/night and rain review; HUD legibility and bounded frame cost. Source inspection alone cannot accept visibility, lighting or comfort.
