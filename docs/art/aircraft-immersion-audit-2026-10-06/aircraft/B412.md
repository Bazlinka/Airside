## Biggest mismatches

**Observed fixture:** the near-side cabin reads as a large plain door plate, with narrow remaining dark glazing. Both sides have this issue. The nose windows look like small dark surface patches; skids and blades read as straight bars. Paint is fitted and coherent, so another livery pass offers less immersion than fixing these shapes.

**Source-derived:** `scripts/generate-air-017-bell-412.py:69–83` makes a solid oval cabin and belly. Lines 136–146 add shallow box glazing and whole rectangular door boxes, rather than openings, frame subdivisions or a hollow cabin. Increasing alpha alone cannot reveal a convincing interior through an intact hull. Glass alpha is 0.68 (`AirsideAdelaideEmergencyAviation.cs:53–57`); sorting/reflections remain unverified.

**Rotor discrepancy to resolve:** generator lines 113–122 describe a two-blade tail rotor, but create two full-span bars at 28° and 118°. Together these suggest four arms. This is a geometry finding; animation was not reviewed. Retain the existing four-blade main rotor and blur rig.

**Variant/mission uncertainty:** catalogue lines 208–215 identify Bell 412EP and a 13-seat utility description. This does not prove medical seating. Bell explicitly offers an upgrade converting the 412EP's original analog displays to glass; do not copy an EPX/EPI glass panel by default. Babcock confirms mission-modified South Australian Bell 412s and Stryker Power Pro XT transfer capability, but these pages do not establish VH-SAR's exact cockpit, stretcher placement or model suffix.
