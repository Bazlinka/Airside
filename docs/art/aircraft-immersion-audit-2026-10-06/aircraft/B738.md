## Biggest mismatches

**NG cockpit construction is mostly generic.** `JetCockpitProfile.cs:15–23` deliberately distinguishes the NG's six displays from MAX's four and fits B738 eye/width datums. Keep that distinction. However, `JetCockpitInterior.cs:149–171` uses one cuboid main panel, straight glareshield and generic knobs, with the same display construction branch as Airbus Classic. The historical layout tile exposes the flat face clearly. A stepped, subtly angled/curved NG instrument surround, proper brow and raised switch clusters would deliver more identity than adding instrument functions. Exact cockpit curvature remains a reference-fitting proposal, not a certified measurement.

The U-shaped yokes are three boxes on a column (`JetCockpitInterior.cs:73–84`), the overhead is a six-by-six repeated grid (`:276–287`), and the pedestal uses common FMS keys and an “Engine master” part (`:250–272`). These read as placeholders. An NG-specific sculpted yoke, throttle quadrant and selective overhead landmark blocks are warranted; functional avionics are outside scope.

**Passenger views are fitted stations, not a fitted 737 cabin.** `PassengerCabinProfile.cs:20` supplies a representative 3+3 layout, 0.79 m pitch and B738 station. `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:39–47` makes flat floor/ceiling, continuous rectangular bins and two end bulkheads only five rows apart. `:92–114` fixes every type's aperture to the same ellipse and approximately 45 mm reveal. That explains the observed ring-on-wall appearance. Window spacing follows seat pitch; the inherited exterior generator instead uses `WINDOW_PITCH = 0.508` (`generate-air-005-narrowbody-737-8.py:197`). This is a source-derived interior/exterior rhythm mismatch, not a measured real-aircraft discrepancy.

**NG exterior identity still inherits the MAX kit.** `generate-air-adelaide-fleet.py:72–83,253–256` scales the 737-8 source, removes lower wingtip pieces and exhaust chevrons, but does not replace the engine with a dedicated NG shape. Boeing identifies CFM56 engines for the NG [R1]. The newer fixture is adequate for a stylised silhouette; it cannot prove close-view CFM56 inlet/pylon proportions. Treat engine fitting as targeted work, not an assertion that the whole airframe is wrong.

## Prioritised proposals

| Priority | Player-visible change and ownership | Impact / effort |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NG panel and brow:** aircraft/family-specific angled surrounds, stepped centre panel, NG guidance-panel silhouette and small standby cluster; retain six screens and current attitude feed. Shared builder supports authored profile shapes. | High / M |
| 2 | **Window seat construction:** shared curved lining, recessed rounded opening, multi-layer reveal, simple shade and understated glazing; B738 owns window shape/station/rhythm. Preserve wing visibility. | High / M |
| 3 | **NG yokes and quadrant:** round/bevel grips, column boot, characteristic throttle/reverse-handle silhouette and flap/speedbrake positions. Share 737-family base with MAX only where justified. | High / M |
| 4 | **Choose cabin fit deliberately:** default representative classic NG-style bins/lining or explicitly select Boeing Sky Interior; sculpt pivot-style bins and indirect LED coves only for the selected Sky fit. No universal Sky Interior assumption or copied operator upholstery. | High / M |
| 5 | **Convincing 3+3 seat surroundings:** soften seat shells, add restraint/webbing and tray latches, PSU vents/reading lights; move artificial section endings outside allowed gaze or extend only visible rows. Shared seat kit with B738 dimensions. | Medium / M |
| 6 | **Dedicated CFM56 close-view fit:** B738-specific inlet/cowl, pylon and exhaust silhouette referenced against the NG configuration, retaining blended upper winglets and smooth exhaust. Preserve fitted paint and exterior miniature finish. | High / L |
| 7 | **Local lighting and camera framing:** shared restrained roughness/edge shading, window daylight gradient and dim overhead light; B738 seat/eye framing includes rim, armrest and wing without changing the shared HUD. | Medium / M |

The first pass should deliver proposals 1, 2 and 3. Decide classic versus Sky cabin before proposal 4; Boeing's Sky description is a configuration reference, not evidence of every 737-800's installed cabin [R1,R3].

## Acceptance for later implementation

Source/geometry review should verify six NG displays remain separate from MAX, dual yokes remain, cabin stays representative 3+3, authored window stations match the exterior ladder, and curvature does not occlude the aperture. Maintain the existing visibility restore lifetime (`CockpitInterior.cs:25–55`) and material batching (`PassengerCabinInterior.cs:75–90`). Record configuration/reference applicability without claiming certified interior dimensions.

Future native and packaged review must cycle cockpit → left → right → exterior → overview on one B738, inspect forward/overhead/footwell and both cabin gazes, and check daylight/dusk/night. Require no dark rectangular corner gaps, interior seam leakage, artificial bulkhead immediately beside the eye, missing wing parts, or exterior left hidden after exit. Check the shared HUD stays readable; cockpit/passenger FOV and exterior orbit are already separate in `AirsideCameraController.Cockpit.cs:64,79–94`. These visual checks and performance assessment were **not performed** in this audit.
