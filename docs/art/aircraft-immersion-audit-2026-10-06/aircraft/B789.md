## Highest-value mismatches

**The signature 787 window is generic.** `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:92–115` hard-codes a 0.32 × 0.44 m ellipse for every aircraft, with a shallow opaque reveal and no glass/dimming construction. These are code dimensions, not asserted Boeing measurements. Boeing confirms large electronically dimmable windows that preserve the outside view [1]. Their absence is a stronger identity loss than missing decorative upholstery stitching.

**The nine-abreast cabin is present numerically but not architecturally.** `PassengerCabinProfile.cs:28` correctly selects representative `3,3,3` groups; `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:38–72` builds a five-row, flat-ceiling box with continuous cuboid side/centre bins and fixed 0.44 m aisles. The fixtures mostly show the window, so curvature and aisle failures are source-derived, not observed full-cabin defects. Airline seat widths/pitch are configurable: the code's 0.79 m pitch is a representative choice, not a certified 787 standard. Boeing's family reference supports spacious architecture, substantial bins and adaptable LED light [1].

**The cockpit has a family label but mostly common geometry.** `JetCockpitProfile.cs:30–31` shares the 787 deck and 1.63 m half-width between -9/-10, with separate fitted eye Z. `JetCockpitInterior.cs:91–131` calls the common shell, omitting one side pillar specifically for 787; `:181–187` uses four front screens plus a smaller lower screen. Keep the useful five-display foundation, but improve screen proportions, pedestal slope, moulded yokes (`:73–84`) and panel-to-windscreen relationships. Boeing's family page confirms large displays and dual HUDs [1]; its older control-location diagram supports visual placement only, not current variant dimensions [3]. Exact yoke contours need a closer approved reference before modelling.

**Variant geometry should not inherit a global longitudinal shrink.** `scripts/generate-air-adelaide-fleet.py:342–348` scales the -10 mesh to the -9 envelope, including all Z geometry. This is a source limitation, not proof of a visible malformed aircraft in the newer sheet. Boeing lists 62.8 m versus 68.3 m overall lengths with the same 60.1 m span [2]. The family deck should remain shared while cabin stations/length differ; exterior flight views expose wing/chord, engine and door placement too.

## Acceptance for later implementation

Source/geometry review should show independent 787 aperture dimensions, nine seats/two aisles, curved shell/bin clearances, retained batching, and shared -9/-10 deck dimensions with separate airframe stations. Match exterior station positions to the shipped mesh, not an arbitrary seat-row index. `CockpitInterior.cs:25–46` hides the exterior shell while `CockpitExteriorVisibility.cs:12–16` preserves wing/engine parts: revised apertures must remain closed around their edges without losing those views.

Future native and packaged review must cycle cockpit → left → right → exterior → overview, inspect inward gaze and maximum look angles, day/dusk/night and rain, window tint/readability, wing horizon and seam/clipping continuity. Compare -9/-10 side views without confusing fictional paint with geometry. These reviews were **not performed** here; no performance or current-player quality claim follows from the fixtures.
