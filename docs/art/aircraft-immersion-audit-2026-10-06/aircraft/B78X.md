Analysis only, 6 October 2026; source baseline `64ca4a23`. The strongest improvement is a recognisable 787 window/cabin section, followed by a shared 787 deck refinement. ## Source findings and genuine -10 distinction

Boeing lists the -10 at **68.3 m**, versus **62.8 m** for the -9, with the same published **60.1 m wingspan**. This confirms a 5.5 m length distinction, not a proportional enlargement of the entire family. [Boeing 787 specifications](https://www.boeing.com/content/theboeingcompany/us/en/commercial/787.html)

`PassengerCabinProfile.cs:28–29` gives both variants half-width 2.745 m, height 7.121 m, representative 3+3+3 groups and 0.79 m pitch, but moves the -10 window station from Z −24.471 to −26.610. `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:38–47` still creates the same 3.95 m five-row box, capped by bulkheads at both ends. That source-derived limitation erases the stretch when looking into the cabin; the window fixtures alone do not show those end caps. Seat widths/pitch are project assumptions, not manufacturer-certified operator layouts.

`JetCockpitProfile.cs:30–31` correctly shares Boeing787 family/width/height and changes only eye Z (−4.30 to −4.65). `JetCockpitInterior.cs:91–131` nevertheless uses the common jet shell, with a 787 exception omitting the side quarterlight pillar. Panel construction at lines 181–187 makes four broad front displays plus a small lower SYSTEM display; a count of five is not proof of accurate 787 panel proportions. Boeing confirms large LCD displays, dual HUDs and EFB capability, but the opened page does not establish exact display dimensions or variant panel details. Treat a precise layout correction as requiring a verified visual reference. [Boeing design overview](https://www.boeing.com/commercial/787/by-design)

The generic window aperture is only 0.32 × 0.44 m (`PassengerCabinInterior.cs:94`), shared across every type; there is no glazing pane or dimming state in this builder. Boeing specifically identifies large electronically dimmable windows, open cabin architecture, large bins and programmable LED lighting. Those family features apply to the -10; an airline's seats and service partitions remain configurable.

The exterior generator retains scaled A350-derived wing/tail/fuselage-attached geometry before replacing windscreens and adding exhaust chevrons (`scripts/generate-air-010-787-10.py:71–98`). Its 68.30 × 60.12 × 17.02 envelope is plausible, but bounding dimensions cannot prove the detailed 787 planform or door stations. The newer sheet should guide targeted refinement, not justify wholesale replacement.

## Prioritised proposals

| Priority | Player-visible change and implementation ownership | Impact / effort |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Large 787-shaped window/reveal with smooth continuous sidewall transition; remove the visible rectangular dark corners. Shared aperture builder, dedicated B789/B78X dimensions and shape. Establish dimensions from approved reference before enlarging it. | High / M |
| 2 | Add subtle layered glazing, a recognisable dimmer control and restrained blue/grey dimmed appearance preserving the outside view. Shared glass support, 787-only styling/state; no new avionics. | High / M |
| 3 | Replace flat ceiling, rectangular bins and immediate five-row end caps with a curved 3+3+3 section and longer perspective. Share 787 cross-section; -10 owns cabin extents/partition and window stations. Extend only visible depth, using bounded geometry. | High / L |
| 4 | Refine the shared 787 flight deck: shaped yokes, panel bevels, overhead switch group spacing, side-console/EFB mounting and restrained HUD frame/combiner geometry. Keep -9/-10 deck common; validate exact silhouette against primary visual reference before changing display layout. | High / M |
| 5 | Fit each -10 eye/window station to its actual opening and local wing datum, then tune seat gaze so both the raked wing and horizon remain useful. Shared camera starts at 65° and passenger yaw ±78° (`AirsideCameraController.Cockpit.cs:64–83`, `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:26–27`); do not solve occlusion merely by widening FOV. | Medium / M |
| 6 | Introduce restrained indirect ceiling/bin wash and sun-side window bounce, softened fabric and satin panel response. Shared material/light infrastructure; 787 family LED palette. Keep tint, surface and lighting roles separate. | Medium / M |
| 7 | Review the -10 exterior wing/nose/door relationships against Boeing plan/side/front drawings; refine only confirmed deviations. Preserve fitted paint and overview readability. Visibility filtering must retain wing, pylon, fan and chevron components (`CockpitExteriorVisibility.cs:12–24`). | Medium / M |

First pass: **1, 2 and 3**. They address the dominant passenger fixture defects and make the -10's greater cabin depth visible. Deck refinement then benefits both 787 variants. Existing shared HUD (`FlightViewHud.cs:19–25,49–106`) needs future aperture/panel occlusion review with UI enabled; these fixture sheets cannot establish a HUD defect.

## Acceptance for a future implementation

Source/geometry review should verify closed curved lining around real apertures, representative nine-abreast/two-aisle clearance, deliberate -9/-10 station differences, unchanged common deck proportions and no mechanical shade added to 787 windows. Check exterior drawing alignment independently of overall bounds. Future native and packaged review must inspect both sides, inward cabin gaze and cockpit lower/overhead angles, with HUD, recenter/zoom, day/dusk/night and clear/dim glazing; confirm renderer restoration on exterior exit and measure the selected interior's actual frame cost. None of those future checks is claimed complete.
