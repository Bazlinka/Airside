## Biggest mismatches

**Cockpit fittings lack hierarchy.** `SaabCockpitInterior.cs:37–51` correctly separates vertically stacked display housings, but its CRT lower display is a circular compass illustration; `:26–34` repeats the same sparse dial vocabulary. The historical panel image shows clock-like round gauges and very broad empty panel areas. The overhead is a regular 6×6 array of identical cuboids (`:184–187`), visibly sparse in the inspected overhead fixture. Preserve the existing hybrid EFIS/round-gauge character; do not infer that every Saab has one equipment fit. C&L’s 340B-331 sheet identifies Collins avionics and provides indicative interior photos, but browser screenshot fetch failed during this audit, so their pixels were not freshly verified. Earlier reference observations remain documented in `docs/art/reference/saab340-cockpit/README.md`.

**Cabin identity stops at seat count.** `PassengerCabinProfile.cs:17` correctly supplies `[1,2]`, half-width 1.080 m and a fitted window station. Rex’s official access plan confirms single-left/double-right seating, with 33–36 seats and configurable toilet location: useful corroboration of the asymmetry, not authority for the game’s exact dimensions. `PassengerCabinInterior.cs:38–64` builds only five rows, flat ceiling/walls, continuous box bins and identical box seats. End bulkheads artificially close this short section even though passenger yaw can rotate fully (`AirsideCameraController.Cockpit.cs:201–205`). This enclosure is source-derived.

**Window and surface treatment is universal.** The aperture uses fixed 0.16/0.22 m radii for every aircraft (`PassengerCabinInterior.cs:94`), without Saab-specific contours. Both SF34 cells visibly show faceting and a rectangular dark surround; the precise rendering cause remains unverified. Shared material creation assigns smoothness 0.2 to every lit surface (`CockpitInterior.cs:60–68`), so rubber, upholstery, metal and lining have limited response differences.

## Acceptance for later implementation

Source/geometry review should demonstrate the preserved `[1,2]` grouping and aisle offset; SF34-specific window contour parameters; continuous opaque lining; distinct display/gauge/overhead groups; and retained wing, engine and four-blade prop nodes from `mdl_saab_340b_v01.gltf`. Preserve renderer restoration (`CockpitInterior.cs:25–57`) and bounded cabin material batching (`PassengerCabinInterior.cs:75–90`). No licensed photos or textures enter the asset tree.

Future native and packaged review must inspect default/panel/overhead glances, both window sides and full passenger yaw, day/dusk/night, taxi and cruise prop appearance, and repeated cockpit/window/exterior switching. Confirm aperture corners do not expose voids, materials remain readable, motion does not clip seats/trim, and the exterior retains its approved silhouette. These checks are recommendations and were not performed here.
