## Design change

- Continuous 40-segment oval fuselage rather than overlapping body blocks.
- High three-station NACA wing with taper and dihedral.
- Lofted nacelles and short attached pylons.
- Six twisted, tapered blades per engine with separate yellow tips.
- Swept/tapered tail surfaces and landing gear connected to its mounts.
- Narrow individual cabin windows, revised windscreen panes and restrained livery stripe.
- Stable part names preserve propeller, flap, door, gear and light runtime animation.

## Validation

- 141 named meshes, 15,596 vertices and 6,984 triangles.
- glTF bounds: 15.09 m wingspan, 3.89 m height and 10.70 m length.
- Repeated generation produced identical SHA-256 hashes for FBX, glTF and binary data.
- Unity Resources import resolves more than 100 renderers at the same metre-scale bounds.
- Packaged universal Mac build visually checked in overview and follow cameras.
- UV-less procedural meshes intentionally use flat lit materials; v06 does not claim UV/PBR texture mapping.

## Licence and fallback

Project-owned procedural geometry with no third-party source or attribution. Runtime
preference is v06, then v05, authored v01, lofted v01, v04 through v01, and finally
the primitive aircraft.
