# 0174 — Simulation-driven human operations

Date: 28 September 2026

## Context

ADR 0114 added seven Quaternius passengers and boarding at stairs. ADR 0116 placed two ramp workers
beside the one player turnaround selected for GSE presentation. That left bridged gates visually
empty, remote jets without a passenger bus, AI aircraft without working crews, and crew figures in
idle poses that did not explain the turnaround.

Creating a second autonomous NPC simulation would duplicate the authoritative aircraft and departure
state, drift after save/load and make accelerated time unreliable. Creating new human meshes would
also spend effort on a solved part of the problem: CHR-003 is CC0, animated, low-poly and already
integrated with stable Unity material GUIDs.

## Decision

Keep CHR-003 as the shared human rig and make airport behaviour project-owned.

- `BoardingFlow` remains a pure timeline. `RemoteBus` distinguishes remote jet boarding while still
  using the stair-truck door rules. A deterministic bus receives the arrival load, clears, returns
  before departure and leaves before pushback.
- Aerobridge passengers are generated by the same flow but drawn only inside a bridge that is at
  least 92% docked. They are not placed on the apron.
- Apron paths use three stable lanes; stairs remain single-file.
- `RampCrew` names the actual task as well as role and position. It covers arrival marshalling,
  safety equipment, fuel, catering, baggage, boarding and pushback.
- Presentation pools a two-person crew set for up to eight simultaneous aircraft. Player teams
  follow `DeparturePrep`; AI teams follow deterministic stand/service windows.
- The existing `Interact` and `Wave` clips are sampled on simulation time. Small project-owned
  equipment shapes make the action readable: wands, cone, hose, catering crate, suitcase and radio.
- Passenger and crew activity never gates the simulation and introduces no persisted state.

## Consequences

- Saves and time acceleration need no migration or NPC catch-up logic.
- The existing nine source characters can look repetitive at close range. Outfit/material variants
  are a later art pass, not a reason to replace the rig now.
- A cap of 60 visible passengers, six remote buses and eight crew teams bounds presentation cost.
- Geometry clearance and actual frame time still require a packaged graphics-on playtest.
