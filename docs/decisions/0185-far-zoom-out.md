# 0185 — Far zoom-out to 45 km

Status: accepted (Unity look not yet verified)

Bailey wants to watch arrivals from far out on the approach. The orbit camera range rises from 4.5 km to 45 km on the
Adelaide field; the classic field keeps 4.5 km. Camera pan radius, clip planes, fog density and the terrain horizon fade
scale with distance through pure functions in `AirsideCameraFeel` (tested headlessly). Beyond the 30 km far ring, an
outer vertex-coloured terrain ring is built from a Copernicus GLO-30 DEM at 250 m (±96 km), generated by
`scripts/generate-adelaide-terrain.py --far`. Open sea has no tiles and is skipped (treated as sea level).

Open items: hard edge at 96 km; possible seam near 30 km; aircraft LOD culling, field tags and mini-map at far range.
