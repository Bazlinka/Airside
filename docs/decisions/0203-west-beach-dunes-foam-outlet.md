# 0203 — West Beach dunes, shore foam and Patawalonga outlet

Date: 2026-09-30 · Owner: Cursor

**Decision:** raise seeded dune berms on the YPAD surroundings mesh just inland of
the OSM coastline (stronger on sand/scrub), draw two combined shore-foam ribbons
named `Coast foam …` so the existing foam pulse finds them on the bare field, and
deepen inland water toward the gulf when it approaches the coast so the
Patawalonga/Barcoo reads as an outlet channel.

**Reason:** Phase 1 landform/coast — the overview coast camera showed a flat beach
dip into stylised water with no dune berms, foam or outlet shape.

**Affected systems:** presentation surroundings mesh and coastal motion collect only.
**Migration impact:** none. Simulation, DEM bake, routes and saves unchanged.
