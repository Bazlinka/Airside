# Terminal doors and more people on the ground

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Decision:** (1) Each walk between the terminal and a regional stand starts and ends at a real terminal door: `AdelaideTerminalDoors` puts one door per bay on the terminal outline's nearest wall edge, at least 12 m from any corner, facing the bay (bays sharing a stretch of wall share a door; six doors today). The fenced walkway corridors and the boarding walk start in front of the door, and the walk begins just inside the doorway, so people are seen to come out of and go back through it. Each door is a visible frame, canopy and sign with two glass panels that slide open while anyone is at it. (2) The next six boarders wait in front of the door for 28 s before their walk (same figure and key, so they simply set off), and a hi-vis gate agent stands at the door while a flight boards. (3) Background people (`AdelaideAmbientPeople`): 18 passengers walking between real OpenStreetMap landside entrances and car-park bays, and staff pacing the airside wall between doors, all pure functions of the simulation clock. (4) The visible-person cap rises from 60 to 110, and a figure's pose is sampled less often with camera distance (every frame inside 90 m, every 8th frame beyond 500 m; background people beyond 700 m are not drawn).
- **Reason:** Bailey asked for more people on the ground, more people boarding and deplaning, and doors, accurate to the terminal, where they leave to board and deplane. Before, walks began at an arbitrary point of wall and passengers appeared and vanished there.
- **Data limits:** OpenStreetMap has the terminal outline and its landside entrances but not its airside doors, so airside door positions are derived from the outline and the stand, not surveyed. Aerobridge gates are unchanged (people stay inside the tunnel, as at the real gate), and remote-bus passengers still start from the bus.
- **Affected systems:** `AdelaideWalkwayGeometry` (corridor start), `AirsidePrototype.Boarding.cs`, new `AirsidePrototype.TerminalPeople.cs`, `AdelaideTerminalDoors`, `AdelaideAmbientPeople`. No simulation timing or save changes (`BoardingFlow` is untouched); no new assets (existing CC0 Quaternius characters).
- **Migration impact:** none.
- **Status:** pure geometry and walk tests pass; the Unity assembly compiles; appearance and performance in Unity unverified.
