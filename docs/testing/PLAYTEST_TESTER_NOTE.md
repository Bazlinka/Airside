# Airside — playtest note

Thanks for trying Airside. Please play for **20–30 minutes** without asking anyone
how it works — the point is to see what is clear and what is not.

## Opening it

1. Unzip `Airside-<build>.zip`.
2. The app is not signed. The first time, **right-click `Airside.app` → Open → Open**.
   (Double-clicking shows a warning with no Open button.)

## What to do

Start an airline at Adelaide and play however seems natural. The airport runs in
real Adelaide time — flights take as long as real ones, and it keeps going when
you close the game. That's it.

## Afterwards, please tell us

- What did you think the game wanted you to do in the first two minutes?
- Was there a moment you didn't know what to do next? When?
- What happened to your aircraft? Describe one trip in your own words.
- Anything that looked broken, confusing or slow?
- Would you open it again tomorrow? Why / why not?

If it crashes, please send `~/Library/Logs/DefaultCompany/Airside/Player.log`.
